Product planning mockups

PinIT Exchange

Two products, one identity: PinIT Hub is private protect / store / track for the owner only. PinIT Exchange is the public marketplace where creators list work and buyers purchase — with DNA provenance carried from Hub into every listing.

Private · Owner only

PinIT Hub

Businesses & creators protect originals, vault them, and monitor misuse. Only the account owner sees this.

  • Protect → DNA + Vault + Certificate
  • Store encrypted originals
  • Track leaks / copies / opens
  • Never public by default
Public marketplace

PinIT Exchange

Photographers, designers, painters, studios list licensed work. Buyers browse, buy, and receive provenance-backed assets.

  • Creators publish listings from protected assets
  • Buyers discover & purchase licenses
  • Sale does not expose private vault
  • Each listing can show “PinIT verified DNA”
1. Protect in Hub→ 2. Choose “List on Exchange”→ 3. Buyer purchases→ 4. License + provenance delivered
